TONIGHT'S TV SETUP - CHECKLIST
==============================

Plan on about an hour. The first build downloads roughly 1 GB of Android tools.


BEFORE YOU START
----------------
[ ] Laptop and TV are on the SAME Wi-Fi.
[ ] Laptop is charged or plugged in, and has internet.
[ ] Download LivingRoomHome-setup.zip to the laptop.
    Right-click the zip > Properties > tick "Unblock" > OK.
    Then right-click > Extract All, into e.g. Documents\LivingRoomHome.
[ ] On the TV, turn on Developer options:
      Settings > System > About > scroll to "Android TV OS build"
      press OK on it 7 times, until it says you are a developer.
[ ] On the TV, turn on debugging:
      Settings > System > Developer options > USB debugging: ON
      (if you also see "Wireless debugging", turn that ON too)
[ ] Write down the TV's IP address:
      Settings > Network & Internet > your Wi-Fi network > IP address (like 192.168.1.23)


STEP 1 - SET UP THE TV (one file does everything)
-------------------------------------------------
[ ] Double-click 2-setup-tv.cmd. It first builds the app (the first time takes a few
    minutes and downloads Android tools), then asks for the TV's IP address.
    Use the same file again any time to update the home screen.
[ ] On the TV: "Allow USB debugging?" > tick "Always allow from this computer" > Allow.
    (Newer TVs may instead ask you to pair with a code: the script tells you where to find it.)
[ ] The script backs up the TV first, then installs the new home screen.
[ ] It asks you to test on the TV before anything is switched off:
      Home button, spinning the carousel, opening Netflix,
      Inputs > an HDMI input, Settings, and the mic button.
    Only answer y if all of that works.
[ ] It then asks about each change, one at a time:
      Google's home screen (switched off, not deleted)
      the new clock screensaver, and Google's photo screensaver
      photo / file / USB player apps
      ad, promotion and tracking apps
    Answer y or n to each. Anything you are unsure about: answer n.
[ ] When the ad settings open on the TV: Delete advertising ID,
    and turn on Opt out of Ads Personalisation if you see it.


STEP 2 - BY HAND ON THE TV
--------------------------
[ ] Chrome: do not sign in. If it is signed in: Chrome settings > Sync off, then sign out.
    (This keeps your phone's history off the TV.)
[ ] Settings > Privacy > Google Assistant (or Account): turn OFF Personal results,
    so the Assistant cannot show your Google Photos or Drive.
[ ] If the TV ever asks which home screen to use: Living Room Home > Always.


STEP 3 - CLOSE IT UP (important)
--------------------------------
[ ] Settings > System > Developer options:
      USB debugging: OFF
      Wireless debugging: OFF
    Leaving these on lets other devices on your Wi-Fi try to connect to the TV.
[ ] Keep the extracted folder on the laptop. It holds the backup and restore-tv.cmd.


IF ANYTHING GOES WRONG
----------------------
Double-click restore-tv.cmd (turn debugging on again first).
It switches back on everything that was switched off, restores the screensaver,
makes Google's home screen the home screen again, and can remove the new app.
